01 HOW WE GOT HERE

BEACON started with one simple thought: what if getting stuck wasn't the end of the story?

We opened our doors to create the kind of learning place we wish every student could find: one where questions are welcome, progress matters more than perfection, and every student gets the time and attention they need.

Today, our little Gaborone studio brings students and patient tutors together. We work at each learner's pace, celebrate every breakthrough and make space for the next question.
